### Core premise

- **Series / Book:** Solterra Bay — Book 1
- **POV:** Cornelius Drake (dragon Curator)
- **Hook:** A murder and vault theft inside Cornelius’s library gets dismissed as “natural causes,” forcing him and his teen intern Zeph to investigate anyway.

### Working blurb (draft)

A courier is found dead in a locked, warded vault in the Solterra Bay Library, and the city Watch is eager to call it an accident. Cornelius Drake, the library’s dragon Curator, can’t let the case die with the paperwork. With Zeph at his side, Cornelius tracks chemical traces, forged narratives, and a widening web of connections between House Doukas, House Kallistos, and the healer who signed the death certificate.

### Central questions (mystery engine)

1. **Who breached the Kallistos vault, and how?** (melted iron seal, sustained magical heat, alchemical reagents)
2. **Why was Elias of Tyre killed?** (silencing vs collateral)
3. **Who benefits from the stolen correspondence?**
4. **Who influenced the “natural causes” ruling?**
