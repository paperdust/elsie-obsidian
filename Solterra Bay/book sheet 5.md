### Locations & sublocations (visual + sounds + objects)

- **Camp Olea (overall)**
    - Set in an **ancient olive grove**. Dust, humidity, silver-green leaves.
    - Sound texture: insects, kids yelling, hooves clattering, rope bridges swaying.
    - Smells: yeast, mint tea, olive dust, chamomile, sugar.
- **The Old Olive Press (camp complex)**
    - A cluster of slightly wonky structures; “rustic” but to Cornelius, asymmetrical.
    - Key named structures below.
- **Stone Villa (the Lodge / guest lodge)**
    - **Pink plaster**, **crumbling**, leaning slightly.
    - **Terracotta tiles** on porch/steps.
    - Interior details that recur: common room, receiving room, “upper library wing,” eastern wing guest quarters.
    - Sound: doors locking, footsteps on tile.
- **Treehouse Cabins**
    - Perched in olive trees “like abandoned nests.”
    - Connected by **rope bridges** that sway.
    - Boys’ treehouse: bunks and a window shutter.
    - Girls’ dorm: triangular cedar-plank cabin and rope ladder.
- **Pergola / kitchen patio**
    - Open-air stone patio under woven vines.
    - Dominated by a **massive circular olive press stone** used as a demo podium / lab bench.
    - Contains **beehive ovens**, workbenches, high shelves.
    - Sound: clatter of hooves, bowls, wooden spoons, bubbling dough.
- **Library tent (temporary library)**
    - Canvas structure with flap and special lock.
    - Interior: **wooden planks on crates** as shelves, **wax-canvas bags** for books, dirt floor.
    - Strong sensory cues: humidity threat, parchment glue worry.
- **Sanctuary Barn**
    - Stone walls, moss on exterior.
    - Contains an unused **dovecote/loft**.
    - Hay smell, old wood; ladder to loft.
- **Dovecote (in barn loft)**
    - Wooden structure with small arched entrances and wire mesh front.
    - Straw bedding; safe containment for Lukas-as-pigeon.
- **Root cellar (beneath Villa)**
    - Stone-walled, dark; “full of onions.”
    - Used as concealment when parents arrive early.
- **Porch / porch table setup**
    - Where Kyria’s courier cage and report appear.
    - The “migrating sourdough” incident coats shutters and ceiling.
