
## Characters

### Cornelius Drake (Library Dragon)

- **Species/role:** Library Dragon; acts as investigator/analyst.
- **Physical description:** Small dragon with **bronze scales**, **wings ~3 feet tip-to-tip**, **golden eyes**, wears **spectacles** and a **bow tie**. Claws click on marble; tail expressive.
- **Notable sensory details:** Often detects an **ozone** scent around Zeph during episodes; has “enhanced/forensic vision.”

### Zephyr “Zeph” Taraki

- **Role:** Cornelius’s intern.
- **Physical description:** Teen; **dark skin**, **black hair** that can show an odd **bronze sheen** in certain light; often looks **pale/gray at the edges** during episodes; pupils can dilate; hands shake.
- **Condition markers:** Headaches described as pressure or *musical*; sometimes accompanied by **ozone/metal taste**.

### Captain Theodoros Melas

- **Role:** Watch captain leading the investigation.
- **Physical description:** Human in his 50s; **iron-gray hair**, **weathered face**; formal uniform (dark blue with silver buttons, city seal).
- **Props:** Notebook and pencil/pen; baton/truncheon.

### Imelda Kassiani

- **Role:** Head Librarian; alchemical/archival authority.
- **Physical description:** Elegant; **silver-white hair** in a braided crown with **copper beads**; formal librarian robes (deep green with copper thread).
- **Behavioral tells:** Calm, precise; smells faintly of **reagents** after testing.

### Valerian (Sphinx-kin)

- **Role:** Lucchesi’s assistant; key witness/suspect.
- **Physical description:** **Tall**, **angular**, **golden eyes**, **tufted ears**, **leonine features**; wears **burgundy scholar’s robes**; hands tremble under stress.

- **Props:** Wine glass + decanter; speech notes at podium; signed first edition.

## Locations (with sublocations + sensory set dressing)

### City Hall — Grand Rotunda

- **Architecture:** Three-story space; dome painted with maritime commerce + mythological allegory. Floor is geometric **white marble + black basalt**.
- **Columns:** Ring the space; capitals carved with acanthus; symbols of Tile Families (wave/flame/geometric).
- **Sounds:** Crowd voices echo; string quartet nearby; later chaos (screams, shouted orders).
- **Key sublocations:**
    - **Eastern colonnade:** Cornelius’s usual vantage; near the platform sightline.
    - **Southern end/colonnade:** Doukas family cluster.
    - **Platform / ceremonial stage:** Podium, curtains, shrouded body under deep blue cloth with silver thread city seal.
    - **Second-floor gallery/balustrade:** Overlooks platform; Cornelius uses it for observation and access.
- **Objects/furniture:** Podium with slanted reading surface; chairs arranged into semicircular “tribunal” seating; oil lamps/chandeliers; signing dais/table.

### Mayor’s private office (temporary interview office)

- **Look/feel:** Expensive, traditional, hierarchy-forward.
- **Sensory:** Smells of **beeswax polish**, **old leather**, undertone of **pipe tobacco**.
- **Furniture/objects:** Dark wood paneling; floor-to-ceiling shelves with legal codes and civic records; decorative antique maritime instruments; substantial **oak desk** with carved maritime scenes; high-backed mayor’s chair; two facing chairs.

### Mayor’s private reception room (key witness holding room)

- **Look/feel:** Elegant but contained.
- **Walls:** Decorated with framed historical maps of Solterra Bay’s expansion.
- **Lighting:** Oil lamps, warm pools.
- **Furniture/objects:** Comfortable chairs upholstered in deep blue; low table; sidebar with untouched tea service.

### Hall of Mayors (gallery)

- **Look/feel:** Long room lined with portraits; dim lamps.
- **Details:** Portraits of civic leaders; bronze plaques and Tile Family sponsorship mosaics.
- **Furniture:** Bench beneath a portrait.

### Administrative warren / corridors / service passages

- **Look/feel:** Utilitarian stone, exposed beams in places; quieter than Rotunda.
- **Sensory:** Footstep echoes; oil lamps spaced out; “air before lightning” smell appears around Zeph during episodes.

### Records Storage (door + alcoves)

- **Details:** Door marked “Records Storage—Authorized Personnel Only.” Used for overheard conversations and later archive review.
- **Props:** Filing cabinets, ledgers, document boxes.

### Infirmary (converted council chamber)

- **Look/feel:** Council chamber repurposed into emergency medical space.
- **Furniture/objects:** Cot(s), side table(s), medical gear, guarded entrance; later a **silver tea service** is used as a negotiation tool.

### Alley behind City Hall (service exit)

- **Look/feel:** Narrow alley; cooler night air; practical exit used during lockdown.

### Library (Solterra Bay Library)

- **Recurring spaces:**
    - Cornelius’s **private study** (upper east wing; quiet; window to interior courtyard; reading stand; cricket bowl).
    - **Fiction stacks** with dark wood shelves; returns cart.
- **Sensory:** Old paper, dust, quiet order.
