## Characters


**Known physical cues (from draft):** These bullets are only what’s explicitly on the page so far. They skew toward clothing, body language, illness markers, and species traits.

### Cornelius Drake (Library Dragon)

- **Species/role:** Library Dragon; investigator/archivist.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Small dragon; wears **spectacles**; wings and tail expressive.
- **Notable habits:** Precise, cataloguing mindset; hoards/preserves rare books.

### Zephyr “Zeph” Taraki

- **Role:** Cornelius’s intern.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Sixteen; “grew three inches” recently (growth-spurt awkwardness); has a “loose-limbed gait.”
- **Notable traits:** Strong empathy; pushes back on injustice; helps with research and field support.

### Captain Theo (Watch)

- **Role:** Watch captain coordinating the Demos case and Gorgon registration.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Watch uniform described as precisely pressed at times; uses scrolls/documents; moves with coordinated patrol presence.
- **Traits:** Procedure-driven; quick to focus on Gorgon suspects; uses patrols/inspections.

### Imelda (Head Librarian)

- **Role:** Head Librarian; moral counterweight to Cornelius; helps with donations and logistics.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Ink-stained hands/fingers.
- **Traits:** Steady, authoritative presence.

### Brother Demos (Temple healer)

- **Role:** Temple healer and archivist; petrified victim (initially) and later restored.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** When petrified: complete stone transformation including robes/sandals/bag; pose preserved mid-reach; stone described as uniform gray with ceramic-like texture.
- **Identifiers:** Keeps an extremely messy **ledger**; runs unauthorized charity care network.

### Sister Photine Kallistos (Temple healer)

- **Role:** Senior healer; supervising Demos; central to hidden quarantine/preservation actions.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Grey healer robes; dark hair in a braid pulled very tight; bloodshot eyes/dark undereyes; scrubs until knuckles bleed; hides bleeding hands in sleeves.
- **Identifiers/behavior:** Obsessive cleaning; intense fear of **contamination**; uses/has access to **quarantine supplies**.

### Mother Ligeia

- **Role:** Blind matriarch of the Gorgon Enclave.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Blind; navigates via air displacement/motion cues; wears simple grey robes.
- **Traits:** Strategic, protective, cryptic; commands strong loyalty.

### Xanthe

- **Role:** Gorgon; outspoken critic of Temple triage; suspected by the Watch.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Serpent hair; leatherworker hands with ink/dye stains; practical clothing (leather vest over linen tunic; trousers); carries blade/spear.
- **Traits:** Anger is immediate and visible.

### Kyra Kallistos

- **Role:** Gorgon from a prestige family; evasive about Temple visits.
- **Known physical cues (from draft):** Silk chiton; hands tremble then still; posture becomes very controlled.

### Niko

## Locations (with sublocations + sensory set dressing)

### Solterra Bay Library

- **Look/feel:** High windows; patterned tile floor; old paper + lamp oil.
- **Key sublocations:**
    - **Reference desk / main reading room** (returns stacks, donation sorting).
    - **Lower stacks + historical archives** (older stone, quieter).
    - **Hidden room behind archive shelves** (Cornelius’s “hoard”; climate-controlled; used to hide Melaina).
    - **Private study** (research table; “secondary hoard”).

### Temple of Althaia

- **Exterior/architecture:** Whitewashed, bright, “cleanliness-first”; courtyards; wide windows.
- **Key sublocations:**
    - **Infirmary** (lavender + antiseptic alcohol; beds with curtains; triage in practice).
    - **Public Archives** (thick stone walls; quiet; ledgers and filing shelves; where Demos is found petrified).
    - **Temple supply storage / western access** (secured; supplies diverted/withdrawn).
    - **Temple Vault** (marble pedestal; emergency reserves; harsh bright lamplight).
    - **Administrative wing / corridors** (quiet, echoing stone).

### Town Hall / Council platform / central square

- **Use:** Emergency assembly; policy announcements.
- **Key event:** Mandatory **Gorgon registration and supervision** announced.

### Gorgon Enclave / cisterns

- **Access:** Cistern entrance through abandoned wine merchant’s cellar; stone tunnels.
- **Feel:** Defensive preparations; guards, barricades; information networks.

### Warehouse district / fish market safehouse

- **Feel:** Brine and old wood; rotting crates; improvised weapons and sparse furniture.

### Harbor / docks

- **Feel:** Brine, tar, fish, working noise.
- **Key sublocations:**
    - Smuggler’s quarter berth for **The Constant Stanza**.
