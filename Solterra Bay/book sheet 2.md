
### Characters

#### Cornelius Drake (Library Dragon)

- **Type / role:** Library Dragon; Head Librarian of Solterra Bay
- **Physical:** Small dragon (cat-sized); **bronze** scales; **wings**; **tail**; **ear-frills**; wears **gold-rimmed spectacles**
- **Condition / physical tells:** Heat-sense; breath weapon is a **tiny blue flame** used for precision work; wings and joints ache after climbing/duct crawling; can get dust-coated scales from vents
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Notes / continuity:** Often describes situations as “undignified.” Extremely order-driven.

#### Zeph / Zephyr

- **Type / role:** Cornelius’s intern/assistant at the library
- **Physical:** Dark hair (often messy); mixed heritage noted as **lamassu ancestry**; frequently disheveled during Festival
- **Clothing cues:** Linen shirts/vests; sometimes shows up without shoes; hands become **torn/bandaged** during cliff climbing
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Notes / continuity:** Anxious, earnest; connection with Sophia.

#### Dr. Anthea Floros

- **Type / role:** Botanist; visiting scholar; journal owner
- **Physical:** **Silver hair** (often pinned, later loosening); **ink-stained fingers**; traveling cloak; practical field clothes; wears a **wooden pendant carved like an oak leaf**
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Notes / continuity:** Mentions memory difficulties. Field journal: brown leather with pressed flower design; handmade linen paper; name embossed in silver ink.

#### Sophia Doukas

- **Type / role:** Tile artist; from the Doukas tile guild
- **Physical:** Dark hair in a practical braid; shorter than Zeph; expressive, confident posture
- **Clothing / gear:** Artist’s **apron** with glaze/pigment stains; carries a **leather satchel** with sketchbooks/tools
- **First appearance:** Chapter 6/7 (Festival Market)
- **Notes / continuity:** Skilled with glasswork and technical specs; direct conflict with Kyriakos.

#### Callista

- **Type / role:** Sophia’s friend (social orbit)
- **Physical:** Not described
- **First appearance:** Chapter 6/7 context

#### Nephele

- **Type / role:** Fishmonger; guest house clerk/worker
- **Physical:** Large, solid build; rough, capable hands; smells strongly of fish/harbor work; wears canvas apron and rough trousers at fish market
- **First appearance:** Chapter 3 (guest house desk)

#### Captain Theo Melas

- **Type / role:** Town Watch captain
- **Physical:** Leather armor over practical tunic; short sword; looks tired / chronically sleep-deprived in places
- **First appearance:** Chapter 5

#### Brother Demos

- **Type / role:** Temple scholar/monk
- **Physical:** Tall, thin, very pale; gray temple robes; carries satchel; precise, cramped handwriting noted in his research papers
- **First appearance:** Chapter 2 (as reported), on-page in Chapter 5

#### Imelda Kassiani

- **Type / role:** Head Librarian (authority figure)
- **Physical:** Ancient elf features (ageless); composed presence
- **First appearance:** Chapter 10

#### Orestes (the hermit)

- **Type / role:** Hermit/guardian; dryad-touched
- **Physical:** Bark-textured skin with growth-ring patterns; hair like thin branches and dried leaves; eyes like deep moss (no visible whites); teeth wooden; robes faded gray; bare feet with roots
- **First appearance:** Chapter 8

#### Kyriakos Vlahos

- **Type / role:** Celebrity perfumer; satyr-kin
- **Physical:** Tall, lean; dark hair swept back; small polished horns; strong scent of **Kyriakos No. 5** (synthetic musk); expensive clothing at the pavilion, rough fieldwear later
- **First appearance:** Chapter 6

### Locations & Sublocations

#### Solterra Bay Public Library

- **Overall vibe:** Cool stone, orderly, quiet; Cornelius’s “hoard”
- **Sounds:** Silence; occasional creaks; lockdown system produces a mechanical/magical “scream” and heavy metal slams/clicks
- **Key sublocations:**
    - **Cornelius’s office**
        - **Objects/furniture:** Desk; investigation ledger; brass emergency panel; ventilation grate; lamp; sleeping alcove behind reference section
    - **Reading room**
        - **Objects/furniture:** Long oak table; chandelier; chairs; sideboard with water carafe and glasses
    - **Reference Collection / Western gallery**
        - **Objects/furniture:** Tall shelving; ladders (used by humans); polished marble floor
    - **Herbaria Medica / Botanical Texts section**
        - **Objects:** Shelf Seven, Row B ("Secret Wonders" home)
    - **Private study carrels (Eastern wing)**
        - **Standard layout:** Desk; chair; locked cabinet; shelf
        - **Carrel Seven (Brother Demos)**
            - **Specifics:** Papers scattered; botanical texts; locked cabinet; ventilation grate above; ambient light through door

#### Sea View Guest House

- **Overall vibe:** Whitewashed, blue shutters; not dignified, not a hovel
- **Sublocations:**
    - **Entry hall / desk** (Nephele)
        - **Visual:** Narrow hall; faded geometric wallpaper; small desk wedged in corner; brass key
    - **Second-floor corridor**
        - **Visual:** Narrow, dim; worn door numbers
    - **Room Seven (Dr. Floros)**
        - **Visual:** Research chaos; papers everywhere; rock samples; field guides; pressed flowers; jars; pencils
        - **Key furniture:** Writing desk; bed; wardrobe; washstand
        - **Audio:** Curtains moving; general quiet
        - **Notable sensory detail:** Strong perfume trace (Kyriakos No. 5) at the desk/journal outline
        - **Evidence objects:** Clean rectangle on desk where journal rested; borosilicate glass fragments
    - **Service alley below window**
        - **Visual:** Narrow; rough limestone walls with handholds; crates stacked like a ladder
        - **Use:** Climb access to second-floor window
    - **Neighboring terrace**
        - **Use:** Sophia sketching; vantage over alley/lantern angles

#### Harbor district & fish market

- **Fish market**
    - **Visual:** Wooden stalls; patched awnings; ice trays; tile counter at Nephele’s stall
    - **Sounds:** Merchants shouting; gulls screaming; children running
    - **Smells:** Strong fish/brine/blood/seaweed

#### Harbor Square / Festival Market / Festival Pavilion

- **Harbor Square**
    - **Visual:** Crowds; central fountain; lanterns; vendor stalls
    - **Sounds:** Music from multiple directions; drunk singing; crowd noise
    - **Key points:** Fountain meeting spot; lantern-lighting area
- **Festival Market**
    - **Visual:** Lantern-lit stalls; tile guild stall with light-reactive glazed tiles
- **Festival Pavilion**
    - **Visual:** Garish purple/gold canvas roof; crowded aisles; Kyriakos’s stall draped in cream silk; tiered shelves of bottles
    - **Smells:** Roasted nuts; woodsmoke; perfume oil

#### Seacliff Villa

- **Notes:** Fancy villa on hill; oyster delivery location

#### Widow’s Perch (cliffs)

- **Overall vibe:** Near-vertical limestone; salt spray; violent wind
- **Sounds:** Wind roar; distant waves
- **Sublocations:**
    - **Summit**
        - **Visual:** Weathered stone; scrub grass; twisted trees; lichen smears
    - **Orestes’s shelter**
        - **Visual:** Driftwood, stones, dried kelp roof; leaning structure
    - **Cliff trail / alcove above tide pools**
        - **Use:** Vantage point; where Orestes was bound; where the team plans/observes
    - **Tide pools below**
        - **Visual:** Rock shelf pools; seaweed ribbons; disturbed pool with measurement marks
        - **Moon-Scented Orchid pool**
            - **Visual:** Tiny plant growing from a crack in submerged limestone; petals unfurl at midnight

### Setting sensory palette (quick reference)

- **Signature smell:** Kyriakos No. 5 (synthetic musk; sweet/chemical; expensive; jasmine/orchid undertone)
- **Signature sound:** Festival cacophony vs. library “proper quiet”
- **Signature texture:** Salt-slick limestone; dust-coated ventilation metal; worn leather bindings
